### ✨ Why This Demo Matters

Most symbolic music generation models rely on massive parameter counts yet still struggle with long-range polyphonic coherence—often drifting into aimless meandering or broken voice leading. 

Trained from scratch on a curated corpus of **7,000+ Baroque scores (~33M tokens)**, this **50M-parameter** prototype demonstrates that embedding **musical pitch and harmonic geometry** yields remarkably structured polyphonic writing at a fraction of standard model scales.

### 🎵 What to Listen For in the Video

In the featured uncurated/selected generation samples, pay attention to these hallmark Baroque contrapuntal behaviors:

1. **Fugal Subject & Tonal Answer:** Staggered multi-voice entries where subsequent voices imitate the opening subject at musically meaningful intervals (e.g., fifth/octave relationships).
2. **Independent Voice Leading:** Distinct soprano, alto, tenor, and bass trajectories that maintain harmonic consonance while preserving rhythmic independence.
